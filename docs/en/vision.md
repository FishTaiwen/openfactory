# Vision

[English](vision.md) | [繁體中文](../zh-TW/vision.md)

OpenFactory is a planned open-source modular manufacturing management platform for teams relying on spreadsheets and manual entry.

**Enter once. Flow everywhere.** Data is entered at its source and reused throughout receiving, inspection, and warehouse operations.

Modules are optional; interfaces are mandatory. Organizations can adopt individual modules or assemble a broader ERP over time. A complete ERP is a long-term goal; finance, costing, tax, and production requirements need separate design.

The first MVP covers receiving, IQC, Excel/CSV exchange, and a REST API. Success means one receipt creates one inspection task, and one authorized quality decision becomes available to the warehouse without retyping.

Future inventory, purchasing, NCR, supplier, production, and shipping modules remain proposals.
